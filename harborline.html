Loan requirements

What every applicant needs, and when a cosigner comes into play.

Every applicant

  • 18+ (19 in AL and NE) and a U.S. citizen or permanent resident
  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Verifiable income (pay stubs, W-2s or tax returns)
  • Active checking account and email
  • Debt-to-income ratio under 45%

Cosigner required

  • Marked with a yellow Cosigner badge on each loan
  • Cosigner credit score of 650 or higher
  • Steady income and U.S. residency
  • Must complete the cosigner section of the application
  • Equally responsible for repayment

Secured loans

  • Collateral details: vehicle, property, savings, CD or securities
  • Proof of ownership or account statements
  • Insurance on vehicles and property

Business & farm

  • 2 years of business tax returns (startups: business plan)
  • Profit & loss and balance sheet
  • Personal guarantee from 20%+ owners
  • EIN and business bank statements
